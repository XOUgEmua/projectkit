About ProjectKit

ProjectKit is a small set of free tools for building the documents that every project needs and that nobody wants to assemble by hand.

The core belief is simple: project management templates should not be a paid download, and they should not require an account. You open the tool, you type your team and your tasks, and you get a clean document you can use immediately.

What we make

RACI matrix generator, Gantt chart generator, SWOT analysis builder and project charter templates. Each one runs in your browser, exports to CSV, and works with Excel and Google Sheets.

No sign-up, no tracking

The generators run entirely client-side in JavaScript. Your team names and task descriptions are not transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly press "Save to database". We do not sell or share personal data.

Corrections and requests

If you find an error in a template, or need a correction, get in touch. Corrections get made.

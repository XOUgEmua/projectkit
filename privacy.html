Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

Summary

We collect as little as possible. The project tools run in your browser and do not send your data to us unless you press "Save to database".

What the generators do with your input

Team names, task names and descriptions are processed locally in your browser by JavaScript. They are not transmitted to our servers, not stored, and not visible to us.

If you press "Save to database", the data you entered is stored in our database so it can be retrieved later. In that case we hold: project name, project description, team names, and task data.

Analytics

We use privacy-focused, cookie-free analytics to count page views. These record aggregated traffic only — no cross-site tracking, no advertising cookies, no user profiles.

Advertising

This site displays advertising. Advertising partners, including Google AdSense, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Data retention and deletion

Saved projects are retained for up to 12 months. To request deletion of any stored data, contact us via the contact page and we will remove it.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, UK, California or elsewhere with data protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. Contact us and we will action it.

Third parties

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data is processed by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and our database provider (Supabase), both of which act as processors under data processing agreements.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be noted with an updated date at the top of this page.
